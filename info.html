About the club

A tree is only as strong as its roots.

Tribe & Tree is a private cannabis social club in Parys, Free State, with its own members' dispensary.

We started the club to give adults a safe, private and welcoming place to enjoy cannabis — away from the street, among people who share the same respect for the plant. The tree is the plant and the place; the tribe is everyone who gathers around it.

We're also part of the Tribe & Tree family in Fochville.

House rules

  • You must be 18 or older. Bring a valid ID or passport every time you join.
  • Cannabis is for members' private use only — no reselling.
  • Consume on the club premises, not on the street or in your car.
  • Respect the space, the staff and your fellow members.
  • Never drive under the influence. Plan your ride home.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is this legal?

South Africa's Cannabis for Private Purposes Act allows private use by adults. Tribe & Tree operates as a private members' club — cannabis is for members only and not for sale to the public.

Do I need to be a member to visit?

Yes. You can sign up on your first visit — just bring a valid ID or passport proving you're 18 or older.

Can I bring a friend?

Friends are welcome to sign up too. Everyone who enters needs to be 18+ and a member.

Can I use the club when I'm visiting Parys for the weekend?

Absolutely. Plenty of our members are weekenders and river-town visitors.
